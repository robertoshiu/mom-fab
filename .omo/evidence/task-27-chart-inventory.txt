FINDINGS
--------
* The brief's "FDC 5x50" is actually 4 params x 50 = 200 pts TOTAL, but each of
  the 4 FDC parameters is its OWN createLineChart SVG capped at FDC_WINDOW=50.
  No single chart series renders >200 points.
* SPC sliding window = 50 (WINDOW); X-bar and R are separate 50-pt charts.
* The single high-DENSITY visual in the app (Yield wafer map, per-wafer defect
  dots) is ALREADY a module-local Canvas (yield.js _drawWafer / getContext('2d')).
* APS/WIP Gantts and the APS heatmap top out at ~100 SVG rects (rows/cells),
  NOT high-density scatter — far below the 200-element concern and well within
  the global <3000-node DOM budget.

VERDICT: No chart exceeds the 200-point Canvas threshold. The only high-density
         surface (wafer map) is already Canvas. NO canvas migration required in
         components/chart-kit.js. chart-kit left SVG-only by design (PASS).
